#  Executive Dashboard: Ontario High School Non-Completion Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project explores the macro-level factors associated with high school non-completion in Ontario between **2018 and 2022** using a PESTEL (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal) framework.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help education stakeholders analyze trends, monitor key performance indicators, and evaluate how political, economic, and social conditions may influence student success.

---

## Business Problem

High school non-completion has significant social and economic consequences for individuals, communities, and governments. While graduation rates have generally improved across Ontario, understanding *why* students leave school early requires looking beyond the education system itself.

Decision-makers need a centralized, interactive dashboard that combines multiple external indicators into one view, allowing them to identify trends, evaluate policy impacts, and support evidence-based decision-making.

---

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze Ontario high school non-completion trends (2018–2022)
- Examine political, economic, and social indicators associated with educational outcomes
- Evaluate the relationship between policy reforms and dropout rates
- Develop an executive dashboard to support data-driven decision making
- Present insights using interactive visualizations and KPIs
