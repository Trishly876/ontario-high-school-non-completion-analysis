# Ontario High School Non-Completion Analysis

> An interactive Power BI dashboard and statistical analysis exploring the political, economic, and social factors associated with high school non-completion in Ontario (2018–2022).

---

# Project Overview

High school non-completion has significant social and economic consequences for students, communities, and governments. While graduation rates have generally improved across Ontario, understanding the external factors that influence student success requires looking beyond the education system itself.

This project combines Business Intelligence, statistical analysis, and data visualization to examine how political, economic, and social indicators relate to high school non-completion in Ontario between 2018 and 2022.

Using a PESTEL-inspired analytical framework, an interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help education stakeholders monitor trends, evaluate policy impacts, and support evidence-based decision making.

---

# Business Problem

Educational policymakers need a clear understanding of the external conditions influencing student success. Traditional reports often present information in static tables, making it difficult to identify relationships between policy changes, economic conditions, and educational outcomes.

The objective of this project was to design an interactive dashboard that transforms publicly available education data into actionable insights for policymakers and education administrators.

---

# Project Objectives

- Analyze Ontario high school non-completion trends (2018–2022)
- Examine political, economic, and social indicators associated with educational outcomes

# Key Insights

The analysis identified several important patterns:

- High school non-completion declined between 2018 and 2022.
- Higher household income was associated with lower dropout rates.
- The post-policy reform period aligned with improved educational outcomes.
- Attendance emerged as one of the strongest practical indicators of student success.
- Economic conditions demonstrated the strongest overall relationship with non-completion compared to political and social variables individually.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, several recommendations were identified:

- Continue monitoring attendance as an early warning indicator.
- Support long-term education funding and policy stability.
- Integrate education planning with broader economic development initiatives.
- Expand regional analysis to identify localized intervention opportunities.
- Continue monitoring socioeconomic indicators alongside educational outcomes.
